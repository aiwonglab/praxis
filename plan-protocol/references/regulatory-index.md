# Regulatory reference index

Companion to `plan-protocol/SKILL.md`. Read it when you need to cite a
specific provision, not on every invocation.

Pointers only, current as of this file's writing. Verify against the current
text with your regulatory office - citations move and guidance is revised.

**Common Rule, 45 CFR 46**
- 46.102 - definitions: research (l), human subject (e), LAR (i)
- 46.104 - exempt categories; (d)(4) secondary research
- 46.108-46.111 - IRB function and the approval criteria; 46.111(a)(1)-(7)
- 46.109(f) - continuing review not required for expedited, absent a rationale
- 46.110 and 63 FR 60364 - expedited procedure and the categories
- 46.114(b) - single IRB for cooperative research
- 46.116 - consent; (b) and (c) elements; (a)(5) key information; (f)(3) waiver
- 46.117(c) - waiver of documentation
- Subparts B, C, D - pregnant women and neonates, prisoners, children

**HIPAA, 45 CFR 160 and 164**
- 164.502(f) - decedent protection duration
- 164.508 - authorization
- 164.512(i)(1)(ii) - reviews preparatory to research
- 164.512(i)(1)(iii) - research on decedents' PHI
- 164.512(i)(2)(ii) - IRB or Privacy Board waiver criteria
- 164.514(b) - de-identification: expert determination and Safe Harbor
- 164.514(e) - limited data set and data use agreement
- Breach Notification Rule, 164.400-414

**FDA**
- 21 CFR 50 - protection of human subjects; 50.24 exception from informed consent
- 21 CFR 54 - financial disclosure by clinical investigators
- 21 CFR 56 - IRBs
- 21 CFR 312 - IND: 312.2(b) exemption, 312.3 sponsor-investigator, 312.23,
  312.30, 312.32, 312.33, 312.40, 312.50-312.62
- 21 CFR 812 - IDE: 812.2(b) abbreviated requirements, 812.3(m) significant risk,
  812.20, 812.35, 812.140, 812.150
- FD&C Act 520(o)(1)(E) and FDA, *Clinical Decision Support Software*, final
  guidance, September 2022

**Registration, funding, and sharing**
- FDAAA 801 and 42 CFR Part 11 - ClinicalTrials.gov
- NIH Policy on Data Management and Sharing, effective 25 January 2023
- NIH single IRB policy for multi-site research
- Certificate of Confidentiality, 42 U.S.C. 241(d) as amended by the 21st Century
  Cures Act
- ICMJE prospective registration requirement

**Reporting standards the IRB increasingly expects to see named**
- TRIPOD+AI - prediction model studies using regression or machine learning
- SPIRIT-AI and CONSORT-AI - AI trial protocols and reports
- DECIDE-AI - early-stage live clinical evaluation of decision support AI
- STROBE and RECORD - observational and routinely-collected-data studies

**Guidance**
- OHRP guidance on research involving coded private information or biospecimens
- OHRP guidance on unanticipated problems and adverse events
